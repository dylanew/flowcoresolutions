01 / About FlowCore

Specialists in CIPP lining, working alongside your team.

FlowCore Technical Solutions provides specialist technical expertise to contractors and asset owners delivering cured-in-place pipe (CIPP) lining projects.

We focus on one thing — planning and installing CIPP liners. UV-cured liners are our lead specialism, but we also support hot cure (hot water inversion) and other CIPP technologies. Most of our work is office-based: design, specification, method statements and day-to-day technical advice. When installations get complex, we attend site to support your crew directly.

03 / Signature technology

UV Cured In Place

A resin-impregnated liner is drawn into the existing pipe and pressed against the host walls. A train of UV lamps then travels through the liner, curing the resin into a structural pipe within a pipe — no excavation required, and the network stays in service.
